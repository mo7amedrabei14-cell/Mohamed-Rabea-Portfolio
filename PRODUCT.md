# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers, technical leads, and humanitarian-tech collaborators reviewing Mohamed Rabea’s work on a laptop or phone. They need to understand what he builds, why it exists, and how to reach him.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that presents verified work in operational analytics, full-stack systems, and emergency operations, then routes a visitor to email, WhatsApp, or LinkedIn.

Success: a first-time visitor can name the flagship platform, see real interface evidence, and start a conversation without leaving the page.

## Positioning

The site is the public record of an operator who ships software from field constraints: Emergency Operations Center supervision plus the EOC Smart Command Center, Power BI operational analytics, and an interactive platform presentation.

## Operating Context

Local Vite + React 19 single-page site. English and Arabic. GitHub Pages may serve it under a repository base path. Contact submit opens a mailto draft. Presentation can be embedded from a public GitHub Pages URL.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual `en` / `ar` with `dir` and document language.
- Real project screenshots and a portrait live in `public/images/`.
- Screenshots of dashboards are illustrative; blurred or concept data is not live operational data.
- Do not invent customers, benchmarks, AI products, or live telemetry.
- Work locally unless the owner asks to deploy.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Mohamed Rabea.
- Binding visual brief: luxury minimal OLED (`#020203`), stealth mesh in dark violet / obsidian emerald / cyber cyan, liquid glass, magnetic chrome, kinetic type, 120/18 spring physics.
- Status line: `OPERATIONAL // HIGH-IMPACT DISPATCH`.
- Voice: precise, calm, field-trained. No hype metrics.

## Evidence on Hand

- EOC Smart Command Center (React, FastAPI, PostgreSQL) with `images/05-operations-overview.png` and GitHub source.
- Power BI / operational analytics with `images/dashboard_blurred_final.png`.
- EOC interactive presentation with `images/pres.png`, live URL, and source.
- Portrait `images/Personal.jpg`.
- Roles, field missions, education, and training in `src/content.js`.

## Product Principles

1. Prove with artifacts, not slogans.
2. Keep every claim traceable to `src/content.js`.
3. Motion clarifies hierarchy; it never hides content.
4. Arabic is a first-class reading mode, not a translation overlay.
5. Accessibility (focus, contrast, reduced motion, labels) is part of the craft.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, visible focus, skip link, form labels, modal escape, and `prefers-reduced-motion`. Body text contrast at or above 4.5:1 on dark glass.
